Equipment Rental (Heavy Equipment & Fleet)

The free base app for companies that rent out cranes, excavators, loaders, trucks, manlifts and generators.

FREE Community & Enterprise Arabic / English

Why you need it

Most equipment rental companies still track which machine is at which site in Excel or WhatsApp groups. Machines get double booked, returns are missed, and nobody knows the hour meter of a crane until it breaks down.

Key features

Equipment register

Asset number, category, make, model, year, serial / chassis, plate, capacity and hour meter.

Rates per machine

Default rates per hour, day, week and month, copied to every rental and editable.

Rental contracts

Client, PO, site, period, mobilization and demobilization fees, with or without operator.

Dispatch and return

Hour meter out and in, machine status updated automatically (Available / On Rent).

No double booking

A machine cannot be reserved or rented twice for overlapping dates.

Schedule and overdue alerts

Calendar of all rentals and a to-do when a machine is not back on time.

Screenshots

Rental contracts with the right rate per machine

Client, site, mobilization fees and a rate per hour, day, week or month, with or without operator.

Your whole fleet at a glance

Available, on rent or in maintenance, with the current client and hour meter of each machine.

Rental schedule

Every reserved and rented machine on a calendar. A machine can never be booked twice.

How it works

  1. Register your machines and their rates
  2. Create a rental contract and add the machines
  3. Confirm, dispatch to site, and return when the job is done
