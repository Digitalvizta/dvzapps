Manpower Supply Contracts

The free base app for Saudi manpower supply and labor outsourcing companies (Ajeer secondment).

FREE Community & Enterprise Arabic / English

Why you need it

Most manpower supply companies in KSA still track client contracts, rates and which worker is at which site in Excel. Mistakes cost money: workers left unassigned, wrong rates, contracts that expired without anyone noticing.

Key features

Client supply contracts

Client, PO reference, site, start and end dates, account manager and terms.

Rates per job position

Required workers and rate per hour, per day or per month for each job.

Worker assignments

Assign workers to clients and sites, with full history per worker.

No double booking

A worker cannot be active on two clients at the same time.

Automatic expiry

Contracts expire on their end date and their assignments are closed.

Arabic / English, multi-company

Works in both languages and for groups with several companies.

How it works

  1. Create a supply contract for your client
  2. Add job positions, quantities and rates
  3. Assign your workers and start the assignment
