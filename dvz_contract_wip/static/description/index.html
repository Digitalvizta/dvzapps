Revenue Recognition (WIP) for Contractors

Recognise contract revenue as the work progresses (IFRS 15), not only when certificates are invoiced.

Part of the DigitalVizta Contracting Suite for Odoo 19. Accounts and journal in Contracting > Configuration > Settings > Revenue Recognition (WIP).

Percentage of completion

Cost-to-cost (direct cost to date divided by the forecast final cost from Job Costing), certified progress from approved payment certificates, or a percentage entered per contract.

Month-end WIP run

One screen for all running contracts: contract value, cost to date, forecast cost, progress, revenue earned, work invoiced and the difference. Adjust any percentage before posting.

Over- and under-billing in one entry

Under-billing is booked as a contract asset (unbilled revenue), over-billing as a contract liability (billings in excess), with the contract analytic account on the revenue line. The entry reverses itself the next day, so every month starts clean.

Expected losses in full

When the forecast cost is above the contract value, the part of the loss not yet in profit and loss is provided for at once.

WIP schedule PDF

The schedule your auditors ask for, per period end.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise.
  • Odoo Accounting (Invoicing is enough for the entries).
  • DigitalVizta Job Costing (and its requirements).
