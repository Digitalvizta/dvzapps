Contract Cash Flow Forecast

See when money comes in and goes out, per project and for the whole company, month by month. The forecast is built from open invoices and bills, certificates, planned billing, retention releases, subcontractors, purchase orders and the remaining cost budget.

Key features

✔ Cash in
Open client invoices, certificates not yet invoiced, planned billing of the remaining work and retention releases.
✔ Cash out
Open vendor bills, subcontractor certificates and planned payments, purchase orders not billed and other planned costs.
✔ Payment delays you control
Set how many days clients and vendors usually take to pay.
✔ Per project and company-wide
Pivot by month and category, graph of cash in and out, drill down to the document.
✔ Always fresh
Rebuilt every time you open it and every night.
✔ VAT included
Cash amounts include the contract taxes, like the real payments.
