Progress Billing (IPC) for Contractors

Create interim payment certificates (IPC) straight from the contract BOQ. Enter site progress, and Odoo calculates previous, current and cumulative amounts, retention, advance recovery and deductions, then creates the invoice in one click. Requires the free Contracts & BOQ app.

Key features

✔ Certificates from the BOQ
Every BOQ item loaded with previous quantities from earlier certificates.
✔ Quantity or % entry
Enter quantity done this period, or cumulative % complete. Your choice in Settings.
✔ Overrun control
Blocks cumulative quantities above the BOQ unless you allow it.
✔ Retention
Deduct on the invoice (tax-free line to a retention account) or invoice the full amount and track retention only. Useful for e-invoicing such as ZATCA.
✔ Advance payment
Advance invoice from the contract, then automatic recovery per certificate, capped at the balance.
✔ Other deductions
Back-charges and penalties with their own account and reason.
✔ Approval workflow
Draft, submitted, approved, invoiced. Approval can be switched off.
✔ Invoice layout
One summary line per certificate, or one line per BOQ item. Auto-post optional.
✔ Bilingual certificate
English / Arabic PDF with signature blocks for the consultant.
✔ Billing analysis
Certified per month, retention held, amount due by contract.

How it works

1In Settings > Contracting choose entry mode, approval, invoice layout, retention method and set the accounts.
2Open a contract in progress and click Advance invoice if an advance applies.
3Click New certificate, enter this period's progress and submit.
4Approve and click Create invoice. Print the bilingual certificate for the client.
