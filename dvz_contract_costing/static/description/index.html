Job Costing for Contractors

Are you making money on every BOQ item? See it per cost type, with the final cost forecast before the job ends.

Part of the DigitalVizta Contracting Suite for Odoo 19. Settings in Contracting > Configuration > Settings > Job Costing.

Seven cost types

Material, labour, equipment, subcontract, site overheads, head-office overheads and other. Set the type once on product categories, products or expense accounts; every cost posted on a contract is classified automatically. Timesheets count as labour, site store issues as material, subcontractor certificates as subcontract.

Budget split per BOQ item

Enter material, labour, equipment, subcontract and site overhead cost per unit, plus budget labour hours. The estimated unit cost becomes their total, so the budget stays one number for the planned margin.

Cost to complete and forecast final cost

Three methods per contract: remaining work at budget rates (from certified progress), remaining budget, or entered by the project team. Open purchase orders are always included, so the forecast never shows less than what is already ordered.

Labour cost from timesheets

Link a task to a BOQ item and every timesheet on it is charged to that item as labour, with actual hours next to budget hours.

Head-office overheads

Add a percentage on top of direct cost in the forecast, and/or allocate the real head-office cost of each month across running contracts by direct cost, labour hours, contract value or equally. One click to post, one click to undo.

Job costing tab and PDF

The contract shows forecast final cost, forecast margin against plan, variance to budget and the items over budget, with a cost table per type and a cost-to-complete list per BOQ item. Print the job cost report for the site meeting.

Job cost analysis

Pivot and chart of budget, actual and committed cost by contract, BOQ item, cost type and month.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise.
  • DigitalVizta Contracts & BOQ, Progress Billing (IPC) and Contract Procurement & Budget Control.
  • Odoo Timesheets (installed automatically).
