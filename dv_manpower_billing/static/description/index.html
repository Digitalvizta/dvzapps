Why you need it

Calculating monthly invoices by hand (prorated days, different rates, overtime at 1.5) is slow and error-prone, and errors mean disputes and late payments.

Key features

Automatic amounts

Hourly, daily or monthly rate from the contract, prorated on days worked.

Overtime billing

Overtime billed with the factor you set (Saudi Labor Law default 1.5).

One-click invoice

Create the customer invoice directly from an approved timesheet.

Bulk invoicing

Select many approved timesheets and invoice them in one action.

ZATCA e-invoicing

Standard Odoo invoices, so the Saudi localization and ZATCA apply.

Invoice tracking

Invoiced amount on each contract and payment status on each timesheet.

How it works

  1. Client approves the timesheet
  2. Click "Create Invoice"
  3. Validate and send the invoice (ZATCA)

Note: Requires the Timesheets app and Odoo Invoicing/Accounting.
