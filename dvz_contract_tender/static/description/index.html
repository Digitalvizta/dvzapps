Tendering & Estimation for Contractors

From the CRM opportunity to the signed contract: price the client's BOQ on Odoo quotations, keep every revision, send a bilingual tender offer, and turn the winning revision into a contract in one click. Tenders never create invoices: billing stays on the contract.

Key features

✔ From CRM to tender
A New Tender button on every CRM opportunity, with the client, project title and deadline filled in.
✔ Priced BOQ on the Odoo quotation
Item numbers, sections, units and quantities, with the familiar Odoo quotation screen.
✔ Cost build-up per item
Material, labour, equipment and subcontract cost, plus overhead % and profit %. Rates are calculated in one click.
✔ Revisions R0, R1, R2
Each new revision copies the tender and locks the old one, so you can always prove what you offered and when.
✔ Compare revisions
Rates and amounts per BOQ item side by side across all revisions.
✔ Bilingual tender offer
English/Arabic PDF with priced BOQ, commercial terms, scope, exclusions and signatures, sent by email or accepted online in the portal.
✔ Bid bonds
Create the bid bond from the tender and track its expiry with the other bank guarantees.
✔ Won or lost
Won creates the contract with its BOQ, cost budget and terms. Lost records the reason on the tender and the CRM opportunity.
