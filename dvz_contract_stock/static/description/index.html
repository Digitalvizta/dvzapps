Site Stores & Material Control for Contractors

Know what material is on every site, where it came from and what it really cost each project.

Part of the DigitalVizta Contracting Suite for Odoo 19. Every feature below can be switched on or off in Settings > Contracting > Site Stores & Material Control.

Site store per contract

Confirming a contract creates its own store location. Purchase orders for the contract are received straight into the site store (optional), so you always see what is on each site and its value.

Check stock before buying

Material requests show what is free in the main store. Processing a request sends what is available from the store to the site and creates purchase orders only for the shortfall. Users can change the split per line.

Transfers between sites and returns

Move material from one site to another, or return leftovers to the main store in one click at the end of a project. The material cost moves with it between the projects' analytic accounts, so each project's actual cost is right.

Material used on site

Site teams record material used and wasted per BOQ item. Stock on site goes down, and every BOQ item shows its material used and wasted cost next to its budget.

Supplier returns reduce the ordered cost

Goods returned to a supplier are taken off the "Ordered" amount of their BOQ item, so budget control stays correct.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise, Inventory and Purchase.
  • DigitalVizta Contracts & BOQ and Contract Procurement & Budget Control.
