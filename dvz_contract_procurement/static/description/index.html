Contract Procurement & Budget Control

Site teams request materials against BOQ items. Approved requests become RFQs and purchase orders linked to the contract, and every BOQ item shows its cost budget against what was requested, ordered, received and billed. Requires the free Contracts & BOQ app and Odoo Purchase.

Key features

✔ Material requests per contract
Request materials against a BOQ item with quantity, required date and site area.
✔ Approval before buying
A contracting manager approves material requests before any RFQ is created.
✔ RFQs in one click
Approved requests become RFQs grouped by vendor, already linked to the contract and its analytic account.
✔ Budget vs actual per BOQ item
Cost budget against requested, ordered, received and billed amounts, with % used.
✔ Over-budget warning or block
Warn on the purchase order, or let only managers confirm orders that exceed the budget.
✔ Pivot and graph reports
Compare budget and spend by contract and BOQ item across all projects.
