Consultant & Client Portal

Consultants review measured quantities and approve or reject payment certificates and variation orders online, with a comment. Clients follow their contracts, certificates and variations in the portal. No more printing, stamping and scanning.

Key features

✔ Approve online
The consultant sees every BOQ line: previous, this period and cumulative quantities, then approves or rejects.
✔ Email with a link
When a certificate or variation is submitted, the consultants get an email with a review link.
✔ Full audit trail
Every decision is logged with name, date, time and comment on the document.
✔ Rejection with reason
Rejected certificates go back to draft with the consultant's comment for the contractor.
✔ Client view
Clients see contract value, progress, certificates and variations of their projects.
✔ Works on Community
Uses the standard Odoo portal. No Enterprise licence needed.
