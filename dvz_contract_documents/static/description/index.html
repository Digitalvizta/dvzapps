Document Control: RFIs, Submittals & Transmittals

Every question to the consultant and every document for approval, numbered, tracked and answered online.

Part of the DigitalVizta Contracting Suite for Odoo 19. Answer and review times in Contracting > Configuration > Settings > Document Control.

Requests for information (RFIs)

Numbered RFIs per contract with discipline, drawing reference, question, proposed solution and files. Due dates and overdue alerts, a daily reminder to chase overdue answers, and a printable RFI form.

RFI to variation order

An answer with cost or time impact becomes a variation order in one click, with the question and answer copied into it.

Submittals with review codes

Material approvals, shop drawings, method statements, samples, ITPs and as-builts. The consultant reviews with A (approved), B (approved as noted), C (revise and resubmit) or D (rejected). C and D create the next revision (R1, R2...) and keep the full history.

Transmittals

Send a list of documents with purpose, revision, format and copies, plus files. The receiver acknowledges receipt online; print the transmittal letter.

Consultant portal

Consultants see what is waiting for them, answer RFIs and review submittals with comments and marked-up files, from any browser. The contract page in the portal lists its RFIs and submittals.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise.
  • DigitalVizta Contract Portal (and its requirements).
