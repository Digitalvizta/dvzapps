Site Diary: Daily Site Reports

Everything that happened on site today, in one approved report, with the labour and equipment cost going straight to the job.

Part of the DigitalVizta Contracting Suite for Odoo 19. Settings in Contracting > Configuration > Settings > Site Diary.

Manpower and equipment

Crews by trade with workers, hours and overtime, own staff or subcontractor crews (counted, not costed). Equipment with working and idle hours, own or rented. "Copy crews from last diary" fills a new day in one click.

Work done per BOQ item

Quantity done today and to date as a percentage of the BOQ, ready for the next payment certificate.

Weather, delays and safety

Weather and temperature, delays by cause with hours lost and who is responsible (contractor, client or neutral) for extension-of-time claims, toolbox talks, incidents, visitors and instructions received.

Photos and PDF

Site photos with captions, printed in the daily site report with the engineer and approver names.

Approval and job costing

The site engineer submits, the contract manager approves. On approval, labour and equipment cost is posted to the contract per BOQ item and appears in Job Costing. Reset to draft removes it again.

Delay analysis

Pivot of hours lost by cause, responsibility, contract and month; the contract shows the claimable hours.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise.
  • DigitalVizta Job Costing (and its requirements).
