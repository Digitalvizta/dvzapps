Why you need it

Revenue looks good, but after salary, GOSI, housing, transport and government fees, some workers and contracts lose money. Without numbers, you cannot fix pricing.

Key features

Full worker cost

Salary, GOSI, housing, transport, iqama/levy and other monthly costs.

Profit on every line

Cost, profit and margin on each timesheet line, prorated on days.

Contract profitability

Revenue, cost, profit and margin on each supply contract.

Pivot and graph

Analyse by client, contract, worker, job position or month.

Find loss-makers

One filter shows every worker or contract that loses money.

Managers only

Cost and profit figures are only visible to Manpower managers.

How it works

  1. Enter costs on the "Worker Cost" tab of each employee
  2. Approve and invoice timesheets as usual
  3. Open Reporting > Profitability

Note: Requires the Billing app (installs Timesheets and the free base app).
