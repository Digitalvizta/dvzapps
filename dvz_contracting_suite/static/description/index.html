Contracting Suite for Construction Companies

Install the complete DigitalVizta Contracting Suite in one step: contracts and BOQ, progress billing, retention and bank guarantees, variation orders and subcontractor management. Built for Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise.

Key features

✔ Contracts & BOQ
Contract value, cost budget, margin, live cost control.
✔ Progress billing (IPC)
Certificates from the BOQ, retention, advance recovery, invoices, bilingual PDF.
✔ Retention & guarantees
Release at handover and after DLP, guarantee expiry reminders.
✔ Variation orders
Change orders with approval that update the BOQ and value.
✔ Subcontractors
Subcontracts, subcontractor certificates and vendor bills.
✔ Everything configurable
One settings page for all defaults, accounts and workflows.

How it works

1Install the suite. All five apps are installed.
2Open Settings > Contracting and set defaults and accounts.
3Create a contract with its BOQ and confirm it.
4Certify progress, apply variations, manage subcontractors and release retention.
