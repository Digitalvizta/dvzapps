Why you need it

Calculating rental invoices by hand (prorated monthly rates, extra hours, breakdown days, minimum hours and mobilization) is slow and error-prone, and every error means a dispute and a late payment.

Key features

Every rate type

Hourly, daily, weekly and monthly rates; monthly rates prorated on the working days of the month.

Extra hours

Hours above the daily standard (e.g. 10 h) billed at the hourly equivalent x your factor.

Fair breakdown rules

Breakdown days are not billed; standby (idle) days are.

Minimum hours

Guaranteed minimum hours for hourly rentals, topped up on the invoice.

Mobilization once

Mobilization on the first invoice, demobilization on the last one.

ZATCA e-invoicing

Standard Odoo invoices, so the Saudi localization and ZATCA apply. Bulk invoicing too.

Screenshots

Amounts calculated for you

Monthly rate per working day, extra hours above 10 h, breakdown days not billed.

One click to a ZATCA-ready invoice

Mobilization, one line per machine and extra hours, with 15% VAT.

How it works

  1. The client approves the log sheet
  2. Click "Create Invoice"
  3. Validate and send the invoice (ZATCA)

Note: Requires the Log Sheets app and Odoo Invoicing / Accounting.
